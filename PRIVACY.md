# Voxli Book Reader Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-09-16**

Voxli Book Reader is a browser extension for reading EPUB and FB2 files locally.

## Data the extension handles

The extension handles the following data only on the user's device:

- EPUB and FB2 files selected by the user;
- the last opened book and its parsed reading content;
- reading position and progress for saved books;
- reader settings and the selected interface language.

## Collection and sharing

Voxli Book Reader does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not
send book files, reading history, settings, or diagnostics to a developer-controlled
server. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

The extension can open the developer and extension websites from its settings page
when the user explicitly clicks those links. Those websites have their own privacy
policies and are not required for reading books.

## Storage and deletion

The data listed above is stored in the browser's local extension storage. The user can
remove it by clearing the extension's stored data in the browser or uninstalling the
extension. Book files are not uploaded as part of this process.
